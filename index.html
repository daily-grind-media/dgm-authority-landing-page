07 Built For Experts

Where Trust
Drives The Decision.

Authority works best when people are buying expertise, judgment, experience, or leadership — not simply comparing commodities.

This Is Built For You If
  • Your expertise is central to why people hire you.
  • Trust matters before someone buys.
  • You repeatedly answer valuable questions your market should hear.
  • You know you should be showing up more consistently.
  • You don't have time to manage the entire process.
  • You want to build authority that compounds over time.
Probably Not The Fit
  • Someone looking only for a cheap podcast recording.
  • Someone who only wants raw footage.
  • Someone unwilling to share expertise or perspective.
  • Someone expecting instant authority from one piece of content.

Especially effective for founders, consultants, coaches, professional services, health and fitness experts, and other expertise-led businesses.
